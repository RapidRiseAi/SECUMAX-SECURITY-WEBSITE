Specialized Services

Accredited firearm training, escorts, recovery and bespoke operations for requirements that fall outside the standard scope. When a brief does not fit an ordinary guarding or protection contract, this is the division that takes it on.

The division

For work that does not fit a standard contract.

A consignment has to move across the country under escort. A piece of plant has to be traced and lawfully recovered. A retail group wants a shrinkage problem addressed from inside the business rather than from the outside. None of these are a guarding contract and none are a protection detail. Specialized Services exists for that work — planned individually, scoped in writing, and staffed from whichever divisions the job actually requires.

We also deliver accredited firearm training for responsible citizens: competency training toward a firearm licence application, together with refresher and safe-handling courses for people who already own a firearm and want to remain proficient. The emphasis is on lawful ownership, safe storage, sound handling and judgement under pressure. We prepare and assess candidates properly — the licence decision itself rests with the authorities, not with us, and we will never suggest otherwise.

Everything in this division is bounded by law and by a written scope. Officers deployed armed are registered for that role and operate within the applicable firearm and private security regulations. Support during crowd or labour unrest is defensive and de-escalation-first, provided to protect a client's people, premises and access — never to interfere with anyone's lawful right to gather or to withhold their labour. If a brief cannot be carried out lawfully and proportionately, we say so and decline it.

What this covers

Accredited firearm training

Accredited training for responsible citizens, covering the law, safe storage and competency toward a firearm licence application. The licence decision rests with the authorities.

Tactical & refresher training

Refresher, safe-handling and decision-making training for existing firearm owners, and role-specific training for the officers we deploy.

Valuable goods & cash escorts

Planned escort of consignments, cash and high-value items, with route selection, vehicle discipline and a recorded handover at both ends.

Asset & vehicle recovery

Locating and lawfully recovering vehicles, plant and equipment, with the tracing work documented so that it holds up if the matter goes further.

Loss prevention programmes

Structured programmes combining stock and process review, officer placement and integrity testing, aimed at reducing shrinkage rather than simply recording it.

Undercover operative placement

Placement of a trained operative inside a workforce or operation under a defined, lawful brief, where an internal problem cannot be seen from the outside.

Crowd & labour unrest support

Lawful, defensive support to a client's site during unrest or demonstration — protecting people, property and access, with de-escalation as the first response.

Bespoke risk solutions

Requirements that do not fit a standard contract, scoped from first principles and staffed from whichever of our divisions the work calls for.

Our method

How we approach it.

Bespoke work needs tighter definition than routine work, not looser.

01

Define the requirement

We start with the actual problem rather than the service you think you need, and establish what a good outcome looks like, who is affected, and what constraints you are working within.

02

Lawful & proportionate scoping

We establish what can be done lawfully and proportionately, what it exposes you to, and where the boundaries sit. Anything that falls outside them is declined at this stage rather than halfway through.

03

Written brief & team assignment

The operation is set out in writing — objective, method, roles, reporting lines, escalation and contingencies — and staffed from the divisions the work genuinely requires, not from whoever is available.

04

Execution & documented handover

The work is carried out to the brief and recorded as it goes, so that what you receive at the end is a documented account you can act on, place before a hearing or hand to an insurer.

Before you call

Common questions.

What does your firearm training actually cover?
We deliver accredited firearm training for responsible citizens: competency training toward a licence application, plus refresher and safe-handling courses for people who already own a firearm. The content covers the legal framework, safe storage and handling, and practical judgement about when a firearm is and is not the answer. We prepare and assess candidates; the licence decision is made by the authorities, not by us, and no course can guarantee that outcome.
How do you decide whether to take on a bespoke brief?
We scope it before we quote on it. If the objective can be achieved lawfully and proportionately, we set out the method, the roles and the reporting in writing and work to that scope. If it cannot, we say so and decline the work — an operation that cannot be defended afterwards is of no use to a client, however well it appears to have gone at the time.
Who carries out this work, and how are they vetted?
Operatives, escort officers and instructors are drawn from our own divisions, are registered with PSIRA where the role requires it, and are screened before assignment — identity verification, criminal record checks and references, with polygraph screening through our own examiners where the sensitivity of the brief warrants it. Every operation has a named supervisor with a defined reporting line.
How is an undercover placement or an escort kept confidential?
Knowledge is limited to the people who genuinely need it, which is usually one named contact on your side. Reporting goes to that contact only, records are held securely, and an operative's role is never disclosed on site — including to your own management or guarding staff unless you instruct otherwise. We do not discuss clients or operations with anyone else.
