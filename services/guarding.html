Guarding Services

Vetted, supervised guarding for sites, estates, retail floors and industrial premises, staffed by PSIRA registered officers who know their post orders. The difference is not the uniform — it is the supervision, the record-keeping and the accountability behind it.

PSIRA Registered 24/7 Operations National Coverage
The division

Guarding fails quietly.

A post is left unmanned at three in the morning. An occurrence book goes unfilled for a week. A patrol is signed off that never happened. None of it is dramatic, and none of it is noticed — until there is a loss to explain and no record worth reading. We build guarding contracts on the assumption that a post is only ever as good as the supervision standing behind it.

Every site we take on begins with a survey and a written set of post orders: what each post is responsible for, what is checked and how often, who is allowed through and on whose authority, what must be recorded, and exactly what an officer does when something is wrong. Officers are briefed against those orders on the site itself, and supervisors verify them on shift rather than over the telephone.

Our officers are registered with PSIRA and graded for the post they hold, and they are screened before they are placed. Because we also run investigation, forensic and polygraph divisions, a shrinkage problem or a serious incident on a site we guard can be taken further properly rather than handed to a third party — and where a client wants independent integrity testing of the guarding force itself, that can be arranged too.

What this covers

Static & site guarding

Manned posts at gates, entrances, yards and buildings, working to written site-specific orders rather than a generic industry brief.

Access control officers

Officers trained on visitor, contractor and vehicle procedures, keeping a legible and accurate record of who entered, when, and on whose authority.

Residential estate guarding

Gatehouse staffing, resident and visitor verification and internal patrols for estates, complexes and secured residential streets.

Retail & commercial guarding

Floor and entrance officers for shops, centres and office buildings, focused on deterrence, accurate incident recording and safe customer contact.

Construction & industrial site security

Cover for sites carrying high-value plant, material stock and a perimeter that changes week to week, including out-of-hours and weekend guarding.

Mobile patrols & guard monitoring

Scheduled and randomised patrol routes with checkpoint scanning, so that a completed patrol is a verifiable record rather than an assurance.

Reaction & response officers

Officers positioned to respond to alarm activations and incidents on the sites we guard, working alongside your existing response arrangements.

Supervision & control room staffing

Shift supervisors and trained control room operators who monitor posts, handle escalations and keep the daily record straight.

Our method

How we approach it.

Four steps between a site survey and a guarding contract you can hold us to.

01

Site survey

We walk the property with you — perimeter, access points, lighting, blind spots, existing systems, movement patterns and the hours of the day that actually matter. Losses usually have a pattern, and it is normally visible on the ground.

02

Post orders & staffing plan

We set out how many posts, what hours, what grade of officer and exactly what each post is responsible for — in writing, agreed with you, before a single officer is placed.

03

Screening, briefing & placement

Officers are screened, registered and briefed on the site itself, including the escalation route and what to do with anything that might later be evidence. Nobody stands a post they have not been walked through.

04

Supervision & reporting

Supervisors check posts on shift, occurrence records and patrol data are reconciled, and you receive a regular report — including the things that went wrong and what was done about them.

Before you call

Common questions.

How are officers screened before they are placed on my site?
Officers are registered with PSIRA and graded for the post they will hold. Screening includes identity verification, criminal record checks and reference confirmation. Where a site handles cash, stock or sensitive material, polygraph screening through our own examiners can be added to the placement process and repeated periodically.
How do I know a patrol or a post check actually happened?
Patrols run against physical checkpoints that are scanned, so a route produces a record rather than a signature. Posts are visited by a supervisor on shift, at varied times, rather than confirmed over the telephone. Patrol data, the occurrence record and the supervisor's visits are reconciled and reported to you.
What happens when there is an incident on site?
The officer follows the written post orders — make the area safe, notify the control room, record exactly what was observed and preserve anything that might later be evidence rather than tidying it away. You are notified through the agreed escalation route, and if the matter needs to go further our investigation and forensic divisions can take it on without the scene having been compromised first.
Can guarding be combined with systems and investigation work?
Yes, and most sites are best served by a mix: officers where judgement is needed, technology where consistency is needed. Our Security Services division designs the access control, camera and control room element, and the same team supports the guarding contract — so a camera position and a patrol route are planned against the same survey rather than by two separate suppliers.
