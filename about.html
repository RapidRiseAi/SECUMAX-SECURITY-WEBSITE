About us

Evidence and protection, under one roof.

INTEGRI Forensic and Protection Services is a South African firm built around a simple idea: the people who establish what happened should be able to work alongside the people who stop it happening again. Seven specialised divisions, one point of accountability, every engagement handled in confidence.

Our approach

Capability first, claims never.

INTEGRI Forensic and Protection Services operates across two disciplines that are usually bought separately. On one side sits enquiry work — investigation, forensic examination and polygraph testing, the business of establishing what actually happened and recording it properly. On the other sits physical risk work — security systems, close protection, guarding and specialised operations, the business of making sure it does not happen again.

Keeping both under one roof is the whole proposition. A retail shrinkage figure is an investigation until it becomes an access control problem. A threatening message is a protection detail until it becomes a matter of tracing who sent it. When those disciplines sit with separate providers, the client carries the cost of the handover — repeating the brief, re-establishing trust, and hoping the evidence survives the change of hands. With INTEGRI it does not change hands.

Work is scoped before it begins. We establish the facts already in your possession, identify the gaps that actually matter, and agree a realistic objective and a defined scope. If the honest answer is that we are not the right people for a matter, we say so at that point rather than after an invoice has been raised. One point of contact stays yours for the duration, whichever divisions are drawn in behind them.

Everything we produce is written to be read by someone who was not there. Evidence is handled under a documented chain of custody. Findings are set out plainly, with the basis for each conclusion visible, so that a report can go in front of a disciplinary hearing, an insurer or a court without needing to be rewritten. That standard applies whether the matter ends in litigation or is closed quietly inside the business.

INTEGRI is registered with PSIRA and operates in compliance with its requirements, and delivers accredited firearm training for responsible citizens. Those are the two accreditations we hold out, and we do not dress the firm in anything beyond them. Discretion is the default position throughout: we work quietly, and we do not discuss our clients.

Operating principles

  • Confidentiality by default — enquiries, files and client identities are not discussed outside the engagement
  • Documented chain of custody — every item of evidence we handle is recorded from collection to reporting
  • Court-ready reporting — findings written to an evidentiary standard, not a persuasive one
  • A single point of accountability — one contact owns your matter across every division involved
  • PSIRA compliance — officers registered, vetted and deployed within the regulator's framework
  • An honest scope — what is achievable is agreed up front, including where the limits are
