Where to start

Which division do I need?

Find the situation that most closely matches yours. If none of them quite fit, that is common: describe it to us and we will point you to the right team.

Money or stock is going missing

The figures do not reconcile, a supplier arrangement looks wrong, or someone inside the business is suspected. Start with Investigation Services.

There is a scene, a device or a document

A break-in, a wiped laptop, a signature that does not look right. Anything that has to be examined and preserved belongs with Forensic Services.

Accounts of the same incident conflict

Several people were present, the versions do not agree, and you need a structured way to test credibility. That is Polygraph Services.

The premises themselves are the weak point

Access is uncontrolled, the cameras do not cover what matters, or nobody can say who was on site. Security Services handles the assessment and the design.

Someone has been threatened

An executive, a family member or a principal is at risk, at home, in transit or at an event. Protection Services plans around the specific threat.

A site needs to be manned properly

An estate, a retail floor, a yard or a construction site that needs vetted officers and real supervision on shift. That is Guarding Services.

The requirement is out of the ordinary

Mining security, a dedicated search, riot and civil unrest control, or firearm and security training. Specialized Services covers what falls outside the standard scope.

Still not sure which division fits?

Describe the situation in your own words. We will tell you which division should take it, or tell you plainly if it is not something we should be handling.
