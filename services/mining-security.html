Division 04

Large sites, valuable material, competing interests

Mining environments combine open ground, high-value material, heavy equipment, contractors and surrounding communities. That mix produces risk that ordinary guarding does not answer. Our teams work to protect people, assets and, critically, operational continuity.

Director-led Confidential South Africa
What this division does

Mining Security

Illegal Mining Prevention Teams

Dedicated teams that identify, deter and respond to illegal mining activity on and around the operation.

Riot and Civil Unrest Control

Support during unrest, demonstrations and disturbances: holding control, limiting escalation and protecting people and infrastructure.

Dedicated Searches

Structured searches of areas, buildings, vehicles and other environments where a focused response is required.

Incident Investigations

Theft, suspicious activity and internal security concerns investigated so management can act on reliable information.

Bullion Runs

Movement of bullion and other high-value mining commodities, coordinated with the operation's own procedures and shift patterns.

Worth saying plainly

Downtime, stolen material and unrest cost more than the security that prevents them. Our mining work is measured on production days protected, not on hours billed.
