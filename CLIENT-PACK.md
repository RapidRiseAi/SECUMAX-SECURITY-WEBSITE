## 1. Three things that block launch

### 1.1 Is INTEGRI PSIRA registered right now?

The site says **"PSIRA Registered & Compliant"** in **79 places** across all 11 pages. The
client has said PSIRA certification is still awaited. Both cannot be true.

Which is it?

- **A — Registration granted**, waiting only for the paper certificate. Wording stays; we add
  the number when it arrives.
- **B — Application still pending**, registration not yet granted. **All 79 claims must come
  off before launch.**

Advertising a statutory registration you do not hold is a false claim, and rendering security
services without registration is an offence under the Private Security Industry Regulation Act.

### 1.2 Who is actually a director?

The site titles **Etienne** and **Jacques** as *Director*. The CIPC certificate lists **one**
active director: **Anri Coetser**. Neither Etienne nor Jacques appears on it.

Needed: each person's full legal name and their correct title. Also whether Anri should appear
on the site at all — she currently does not.
