Forensic Services

Forensic support at INTEGRI is aligned to investigative requirements rather than offered as a fixed menu. It is structured case work on evidence-focused matters, and the exact scope is defined around the assignment and the information available.

PSIRA Registered & Compliant Confidential 24/7 Operations
What this division does

Aligned to the investigation it serves.

Forensic Services is not published here as a standing catalogue of technical disciplines. It is the support function that sits alongside an investigation when a matter turns on the material itself rather than on the accounts people give of it, and what that support consists of depends on the assignment in front of it.

In practice that means structured case work. A matter is taken as a whole: what is already held, what still needs to be established, what information is genuinely available, and what can be done with it lawfully and proportionately. The forensic component is then defined against that picture rather than against a list, and it is agreed with you before anything begins.

We would rather draw the boundary than blur it. Nothing on this page should be read as a claim to a particular technical discipline; the capability that applies to your matter is confirmed when the scope is set, and where a matter needs a competence we do not hold, we say so plainly instead of stretching to cover it.

How the scope is set

Every forensic engagement starts with the same conversation: what happened, what is held, what needs to be shown, and what the available information will actually support. The outcome of that conversation is the scope: a written statement of what will be done and what will not, agreed before work starts and revisited only with you.

How we approach it

Scope first, then work.

Forensic support is defined against the matter it belongs to, never against a standard list.

01

Confidential briefing

You describe the matter, what you already hold and what needs to be established. Nothing is committed to at this stage and nothing discussed leaves the room. If the matter would be better handled elsewhere, we say so at this point.

02

What is actually available

We establish what information genuinely exists, what condition it is in and who has already handled it. That determines what forensic support is realistic, and, just as often, whether it is worth doing at all.

03

Scope agreed in writing

The forensic component is written down before it starts: what will be done, by whom, within what limits, and what it will not cover. You agree that scope, and it changes only with you.

04

Case work and handover

The work runs alongside the investigation it belongs to, under one point of contact. You receive written findings that keep what the material supports separate from what it does not, and separate again from what remains inconclusive.

Common questions

What clients usually ask first.

If your question is not answered here, put it to us directly, the answer will be a straight one.

What forensic support can INTEGRI actually provide?
Forensic support is aligned to investigative requirements rather than offered as a fixed list. The exact scope is defined around the assignment and the information available, and it is confirmed with you in writing before any work begins. Where a matter needs a discipline or a competence we do not hold, we will tell you that instead of stretching to cover it.
Why is there no itemised list of forensic services on this page?
Because we publish only what we can stand behind. Individual forensic disciplines each imply a specific qualification, and we do not advertise one until it is confirmed. What is described here (structured case work on evidence-focused matters, scoped per assignment) is what this division does, and we would rather be exact about that than impressive about it.
How does this division work with an investigation?
From the same file, under one point of contact. The investigation establishes what needs to be shown; the forensic component addresses the part of the matter that turns on the material itself. Because the two are scoped against each other rather than in isolation, neither runs beyond what the other actually needs.
Who sees the material and the findings?
You do, and anyone you instruct us to release them to. Working files are held securely and are not discussed outside the team assigned to the matter and the director accountable for it. We do not name or describe clients to anyone, and we do not discuss one engagement in the course of another.
