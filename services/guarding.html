Guarding Services

The division

Guarding, scoped to the assignment.

Guarding Services is a primary INTEGRI service division. It is not offered as a single fixed product, because no two requirements are alike: the guarding function is tailored to the site it has to cover, the assignment it is being asked to perform, and the risk environment around both. What that means in practice — the shape of the deployment, the hours it runs, the grading of the personnel on it — is settled against your requirement rather than assumed in advance.

Our personnel are registered with PSIRA and deployed under the grading the assignment requires. Scope is agreed in writing before anyone is placed, so that you and we are working to the same definition of what the guarding function is responsible for — and, just as importantly, what it is not.

Guarding also does not have to stand alone. Where a requirement extends beyond a static security presence, an assignment can be integrated with INTEGRI's other capabilities — protection, our specialised mining and public-order work, investigation, polygraph examination or training — under a single point of accountability rather than a second supplier and a second conversation.

Our method

How we approach it.

Four steps between a requirement and a guarding assignment defined in writing.

01

Understand the requirement

We start with the three things that shape everything else: the site, the assignment the guarding function is being asked to perform, and the risk environment around both. What has been happening, what has to be protected, and what a good outcome looks like to you.

02

Scope the guarding function

We set out what the assignment covers and what it does not, what it takes to run it, and where it should draw on another INTEGRI division — in writing and agreed with you, before anyone is placed.

03

Screening, briefing & deployment

Personnel are registered with PSIRA, screened before assignment and briefed against the agreed scope on the site itself — including the escalation route and what to do with anything that might later be evidence. Nobody is deployed onto an assignment they have not been walked through.

04

Supervision & review

The assignment is supervised against the scope that was agreed, and reviewed as the site, the assignment or the risk environment changes — re-scoped with you where it needs to be, rather than left running because it is already in place.

Before you call

Common questions.

What does INTEGRI's guarding division actually cover?
Guarding is one of our primary divisions, and it is scoped per assignment rather than sold as a fixed package. The guarding function is tailored to the site it has to cover, the assignment it is being asked to perform and the risk environment around both — so the honest answer is that we define it with you, in writing, before anything is deployed.
How is guarding personnel screened before deployment?
Personnel are registered with PSIRA and graded for the assignment they are deployed on. Screening before placement includes identity verification, criminal record checks and reference confirmation. Where the assignment warrants it, vetting and polygraph examination through our own divisions can be added and repeated.
Can a guarding assignment be combined with your other services?
Yes — that is usually the point. Where a requirement extends beyond a static security presence, the same assignment can draw on our other divisions: executive close protection, our specialised mining and public-order capability, or an investigation into something that has happened on the site. It runs under one point of accountability rather than as a second supplier and a second conversation.
What happens when the risk environment changes?
The scope is reviewed rather than left running. Guarding is tailored to the site, the assignment and the risk environment, so when any of those three moves, the deployment should move with it. We would rather re-scope an assignment with you — in either direction — than keep a standing arrangement in place because it is already there.
