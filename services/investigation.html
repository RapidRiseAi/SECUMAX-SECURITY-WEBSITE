Investigation Services

We establish what actually happened — quietly, lawfully and in a form that can be relied on afterwards. Every enquiry is run on evidence rather than assumption, and written up so that a reader who was not there can follow it.

What this division does

Turning suspicion into something you can act on.

Special Investigations is the core of this division. A matter usually reaches us as a question rather than as an allegation: someone or something needs to be located, a person's background needs to be verified before a decision is taken, or a situation has escalated to the point where threats are being made and the client needs both an enquiry and a response. The work is to establish what can actually be shown, and to set it out in a form that can be relied on afterwards.

We work from evidence rather than assumption. Each enquiry opens with an assessment of what is already known, what can realistically be established, and what the available information will support. Methods are chosen because they are proportionate and lawful, and we will say plainly when a line of enquiry is unlikely to produce anything usable rather than run up work that leads nowhere. What this division takes on is set out below; if a matter falls outside it, we say so instead of stretching to cover it.

Discretion is a working requirement here, not a courtesy. Enquiries are structured so that the subject does not become aware of them, so that uninvolved people are not drawn in, and so that nothing done during the enquiry compromises the process that may follow. Material we obtain is recorded with its source and the date it was obtained, and findings are written so that what is evidenced is kept separate from what is inference.

What this covers

Track and Trace

Locating individuals, assets or information relevant to an investigation or security matter. What is done, and how, is set by what needs to be found and by what can lawfully be established.

Vetting

Background and integrity screening to support informed decisions on employees, contractors or other individuals. Carried out with consent and within the applicable data protection requirements.

Criminal Record Checks

Background screening for relevant criminal-history information. Requested with the subject's consent, on its own or as part of a wider vetting process.

Extortion Cases

Investigative and operational support where threats, intimidation or extortion are involved. The enquiry and the response are planned together and kept under one point of contact.

Evictions

Security and operational support during lawful eviction procedures. Planned around the site, the people present and the terms of the order being carried out.

Kidnap and Ransom Cases

Specialist investigative and operational support in kidnapping and ransom-related incidents. Handled in confidence, with the family or the organisation kept at the centre of every decision.

Polygraphs

Examination support for investigations, vetting and other verification requirements. Arranged through our Polygraph Services division, on its own or as part of a wider enquiry.

How we approach it

Method before movement.

An enquiry that is planned properly is quieter, shorter and far harder to challenge afterwards.

01

Confidential briefing

You describe what has happened and what you already hold. Nothing is committed to at this stage and nothing discussed leaves the room. If the matter would be better handled elsewhere, we say so at this point.

02

Scope and investigation plan

We agree what the enquiry is trying to establish, which methods are proportionate to it, and where the boundaries lie. You know what will be done, and what will not be done, before any work begins.

03

Enquiry and verification

Investigators work to the agreed plan — tracing, screening, record checks and interviews, depending on what the matter needs. Every item obtained is recorded with its source, the date it was obtained and the person who took possession of it.

04

Findings and handover

You receive a written report setting out the material obtained, the findings it supports and the limits of both. Where the matter continues into a disciplinary process or a complaint to the authorities, we support that process.

Common questions

What clients usually ask first.

If your question is not answered here, put it to us directly — the answer will be a straight one.

What kinds of matters does this division take on?
Track and trace, vetting, criminal record checks, extortion cases, evictions, kidnap and ransom cases, and polygraph examination support. That is the full list of what this division offers, and it is deliberately the full list — if a matter falls outside it, outside our lawful mandate, or belongs with the police rather than a private investigator, we will tell you so rather than take the instruction.
Can you work alongside our attorneys or internal HR team?
Yes, and it usually produces a better result. Where an attorney is instructed we take direction on what needs to be established and how the material should be presented. Where an internal disciplinary process is running, we work to its evidentiary requirements so that the findings can be used in the hearing without further work.
Who at INTEGRI has access to the file?
Only the investigators assigned to the matter and the director accountable for it. Material we obtain is recorded with its source and held securely; files are not discussed across engagements, staff outside the assigned team are not briefed on the detail, and we do not name or describe clients to anyone. Where a second division becomes involved, that is done on your instruction and on a need-to-know basis.
Do vetting and criminal record checks need the subject's consent?
Yes. Background and integrity screening, including criminal record checks, is carried out with the consent of the person concerned and within the applicable data protection requirements. That is a condition of the work, not an optional step, and it is the reason the results can be relied on in an appointment or access decision afterwards.
