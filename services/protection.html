Protection Services

Close protection for principals, families and executives, planned around the actual threat rather than a fixed template. The work is low-profile by design — the objective is that nothing happens, and that nobody notices the team that made sure of it.

PSIRA Registered 24/7 Operations Confidential
The division

Protection is a planning discipline.

Before an officer is assigned, we want to understand who you are, what makes you a target, where you are predictable, and who else is exposed — a spouse, a child, a personal assistant, a driver. That assessment shapes everything that follows: how many officers, whether they are deployed armed, what the vehicle looks like, which routes are used, and what happens on the day something goes wrong.

Our close protection officers are registered with PSIRA and deployed under the grading the work requires. Where a detail is deployed armed, the officers concerned are registered for that role and operate within the applicable firearm and private security regulations, in a strictly defensive capacity. Most of what they do is unremarkable — arriving early, checking a venue, holding a door, watching a room, driving a route that was chosen for a reason.

We prefer a low profile. A protection team that announces itself changes the way a principal is treated and often attracts exactly the attention it was engaged to avoid. Plain vehicles, business dress and quiet coordination do more for a client's safety than a visible escort. Everything is documented — the assessment, the plan and the daily log — so that you can see what was done and why, and so that the arrangement can be reviewed as the risk changes.

What this covers

Close protection officers

Trained, PSIRA registered officers assigned to an individual principal, working to a written protection plan rather than an improvised routine.

Executive & corporate protection

Cover for directors, senior managers and visiting staff, coordinated with your own security, travel and office arrangements rather than layered on top of them.

Family & residential protection

Protection that extends to a spouse, children and the home address, covering the school run and the routine movements that make a household predictable.

Secure chauffeur & transport

Vetted, protection-trained drivers with route planning and vehicle discipline, so that the journey itself is not the weakest point in the day.

Travel risk management & advance work

Advance reconnaissance of routes, venues and accommodation, with a written briefing on local conditions before the principal arrives.

Event & dignitary protection

Venue assessment, arrival and departure planning and discreet close cover for functions, conferences and public appearances.

Threat & vulnerability assessment

A structured written assessment of who or what poses a risk, where you are exposed, and the practical measures that reduce that exposure.

Female close protection officers

Female officers for principals and families who need close cover in settings where a male officer cannot reasonably be present.

Our method

How we approach it.

A protection detail is only as good as the assessment it was built on.

01

Confidential briefing

We meet privately and establish who needs protecting, what has already happened, and what you are genuinely worried about. Nothing is committed to at this stage, and nothing leaves the room.

02

Threat & vulnerability assessment

We assess the principal's exposure — routine, residence, workplace, travel pattern, public profile and the people around them — and write the findings down so they can be challenged and revisited.

03

Protection plan & team selection

The assessment determines the size of the detail, whether officers are deployed armed, the vehicle arrangement, the routes and venues, and the contingency actions everyone rehearses before the first shift.

04

Deployment & review

The detail works to the plan and logs each day. We review it at agreed intervals and whenever the threat picture changes, and adjust it rather than leave a standing arrangement running unexamined.

Before you call

Common questions.

What does a close protection detail actually do day to day?
Most of the work is preparation and observation: checking a venue before the principal arrives, planning and driving routes, controlling arrival and departure points, staying close in public spaces and keeping the operations contact informed. Physical intervention is the last resort, not the plan — a detail that has to intervene has usually already been out-planned.
How are protection officers selected and vetted?
Officers are registered with PSIRA and graded for the role. Before deployment they are screened — identity verification, criminal record checks and reference confirmation — and where the assignment warrants it we use our own polygraph and background screening divisions as well. Selection also considers fit: a family detail and a corporate detail call for very different temperaments.
Are protection officers deployed armed?
Some details are and some are not; it depends on the threat and vulnerability assessment. Where officers are deployed armed, they are registered and competent for that role and operate within the applicable firearm and private security regulations, in a defensive capacity only. An armed detail is a considered decision recorded in the protection plan, never a default setting.
Who knows that a detail is in place, and how is that kept confidential?
Knowledge is limited to the people who need it — usually the principal, one named contact and the officers assigned. Assessments, plans and daily logs are held securely and released only to you. We do not discuss clients, we do not name them as references, and officers are briefed that the arrangement itself is confidential information.
