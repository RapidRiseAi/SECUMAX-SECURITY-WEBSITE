Who we are

One team, from first report to final finding.

Most security problems do not arrive neatly labelled. A name that needs tracing turns into a vetting exercise. A threatening message turns into a protection detail. INTEGRI Forensic and Protection Services was built so that a client does not have to assemble three different providers to deal with one problem.

We combine investigative and forensic capability — the work of establishing what actually happened and documenting it properly — with physical security and protection, the work of making sure it does not happen again. The same team that gathers the evidence can secure the site, guard the premises and protect the people involved.

We are PSIRA registered and compliant, and we also deliver accredited firearm training for responsible citizens. Every engagement is handled in confidence, and every finding is documented to a standard that will survive a disciplinary hearing, an insurer's review or a courtroom.

  • Investigation and protection under a single point of accountability
  • Every item we handle recorded with its source and the date it was obtained
  • Written findings a reader who was not there can follow
  • Discretion by default — we work quietly and we do not discuss our clients

How we work

A process built to stand up later.

Whether it is an investigation or a protection detail, the sequence is the same.

01

Confidential consultation

You tell us what has happened or what you are worried about. Nothing is committed to and nothing leaves the room. If we are not the right people for it, we say so.

02

Assessment & scope

We establish the facts we already have, the gaps that matter, and the realistic objective. You get a defined scope and a clear picture of what it involves before anything begins.

03

Deployment

The right division takes it on — investigators, forensic examiners, polygraph examiners, protection officers or guarding teams — with one point of contact who stays yours throughout.

04

Documented findings

Material is recorded with its source and the date it was obtained, and findings are written up properly — clear enough to act on and set out so a reader who was not there can follow them.

05

Follow-through

Findings usually point to something that needs fixing. We can close the gap — security systems, guarding, protection or training — rather than handing you a report and walking away.
